# Data-integrity audit — 2026-10-15

An adversarial pass over everything the dashboard displays, run against the brief "find where it shows something wrong, stale, fabricated, misleading or contradictory; fix it with tests". Each finding is labelled VERIFIED (reproduced by running the code or checking the primary source), LIKELY (follows from the code, but not observed at runtime) or UNVERIFIED (needs ground truth this audit could not get).

Commits: `a2a8d3e` → `e0a598d` on `main` (12 commits, one class of bug each). The suite grew from 1,105 to 1,316 tests; typecheck clean.

**Five worst (all VERIFIED, all fixed):**

1. **Live ACLED battles were deleted in Khartoum, Goma and Gaza** (`hooks/useCrises.ts`, `mergeWithBaseline`). Hand-entered "baseline" markers were merged *first*, and any event of the same type within 0.5° was dropped. ACLED "Battles" map to `war`, and the `war` markers sit on Khartoum, Goma and the Gaza Strip. Every live battle there was replaced by one synthetic marker dated today. Every count that skips the baseline (the brief, the trend engine, the AI context) then saw **zero** events in three of the world's most active conflict zones.
2. **An OpenSky outage read as 29 airspace closures** (`services/notam.ts`). An empty flight feed made every FIR with a baseline "effectively closed — 0 aircraft", severity CRITICAL. An existing test asserted exactly this. The baselines (~5, ~8, ~250) are hand-set constants with no hour-of-day adjustment.
3. **Wrong-country joins** (`worldbank.ts`, `tradeStats.ts`, `countryBrief.ts`, `trajectory.ts`, `infrastructure.ts`, `HumanitarianPanel.tsx`). The UK/Ukraine fix covered only the drawer. Elsewhere: Niger's trade profile showed **Nigeria's**, North Korea's showed **South Korea's**, Somaliland resolved to **Mali**, Congo-Brazzaville to the **DRC**, "Sudan" matched South Sudan's crises, and Romanian news boosted Oman's trend score.
4. **The AI analyst was told to hide what it couldn't see** (`services/openrouter.ts`). Outages, quakes, fires, GDACS, airspace, vessels, GPS jamming and headlines were all absent from its context, but only flights and ACLED were marked NOT MEASURED. The "Cyber" quick prompt asks about outage patterns. The prompt also said "Do NOT mention [source outages], refuse, or caveat".
5. **Fabricated defaults presented as data.** All ~35,000 WRI power plants said OPERATIONAL; the dataset has no status column (header checked). Missing Finnhub fields became "0.0%", and every quote was stamped with the fetch time. A Boeing 777F (AeroLogic `BOX…`) was badged PASSENGER because any airline-shaped callsign defaulted to it.

## 5. What could not be checked, and why

- **The running UI.** No browser session was available, so the "N issues" badge, "OBSERVE 0/6" and the panels were diagnosed from code and endpoint checks, not from screenshots. On the deployed site, FIRMS (now fixed) and EIA (key pending, counted as down) are guaranteed red entries. ACLED is red wherever its secrets are missing (the GitHub Actions secrets are; see TODO.md).
- **ACLED** was not called: the account had a `flood_user_blocked` lockout, and another login could extend it. The cap's effect (C4) is therefore LIKELY, not measured; the response's sort order is unknown.
- **AI eval runs.** The v2 set was not run: it sends dashboard contexts to OpenRouter on the owner's key, which is the owner's decision. The prompt change (source tags, health wording) is a new arm, not yet measured.
- **Spot checks.** I checked 9 World Bank values against the live API (UA/IL/DZ military spend 2022–2025, UA personnel, DE/UA growth, SY CPI, UA capital), plus 12 endpoints for status and CORS. I did not reach ≥5 per workspace: market, airspace and vessel values need the live app, and curated datasets need their named sources.
- **LinkedIn captions:** not in the repo, and the LinkedIn connector is not authorised. The three quoted claims were assessed as given in the brief.
- **ICAO Doc 8585** (cargo designators) and **IAEA PRIS** (reactor status): not accessible here (D3, J3).

## 6. Residual risks for a skeptical quant reader

- **The trend engine's inputs are still shaky.** ACLED is capped at 500 rows per poll, same-type events within 0.5° are thinned (F3), and the backtest scores a different series from the one shipped. "Rising / falling" is a heuristic on a truncated, thinned sample until the cap is lifted (paginate, or poll daily aggregates as the recorder does) and the backtest uses the same pipeline.
- **The forecasts lose to naive** for inflation and, three years out, for unemployment, and to the IMF on GDP. That is honestly stated, but anyone reading only the chart will see a confident forecast line.
- **Airspace still uses hand-set references.** The labels are now honest, but the feature has no measured baseline. Either record counts by FIR × hour × weekday (the recorder could), or remove it.
- **Curated layers** (311 military sites, personnel counts, nuclear statuses) are not individually re-verified, and some are known stale (Belgian reactors).
- **The AI layer is unevaluated** beyond one 20-answer A/B. The new prompt has had no eval at all.
- **Client-bundled keys** (AISStream, NewsAPI) and **people-search links** are what a security-minded reviewer finds in five minutes.

### Proposals not implemented

1. Measured airspace baselines: the daily recorder samples OpenSky counts per FIR hourly. After 4+ weeks, use the median by hour-of-week, show *n*, and alert only where *n* ≥ 20 and the hour's coverage is non-zero.
2. Source health: give "not configured" (EIA 503 `not_configured`, missing optional keys) its own grey state, so the red badge means "failing".
3. ACLED: paginate past 500 rows, or switch the live trend to the recorder's daily aggregate, and make the backtest read exactly what the live flag reads.
4. Proxies for hexdb/planespotters (keyless) and AISStream/NewsAPI (keyed), so no key ships in the bundle.
